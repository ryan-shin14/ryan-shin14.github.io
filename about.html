Biography

Ryan Shin is a sophomore at Duke University pursuing a B.S.E. in Electrical and Computer Engineering and Computer Science, with an emphasis in robotics and software. He is a humanoid robot researcher at the General Robotics Lab, where he built the electrical stack and low-level motor infrastructure for a 31-DoF humanoid, and he is a co-author of Visible-Reachable Workspace for Perception-Aware Humanoid Design. He was born and raised in Auburn, Georgia.

Interests

  • Robot electronics, power systems and hardware design
  • Control, simulation and learned locomotion policies
  • Embedded and full-stack software
